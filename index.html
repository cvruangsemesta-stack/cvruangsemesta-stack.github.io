ABOUT ALFA SENTRA WISESA

Connecting Indonesian supply with international demand.

PT. Alfa Sentra Wisesa is an Indonesia-based trading and export company focused on charcoal and carbon products. We work with supply partners and buyers to coordinate product specifications, preparation, packaging and international shipment.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the buyer's specification, confirm the supply, prepare the goods and documentation, and coordinate delivery for export.
